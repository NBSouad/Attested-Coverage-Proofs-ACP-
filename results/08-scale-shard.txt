=== ACP STARK: sharded (recursive-composition) scaling ===
Sharded proving of a 2^18-permutation workload as 4 independent shard(s) of 2^16 perms:
  shard  0: prove   15.71 s  proof   279.2 KB  ~mem   0.9 GB
  shard  1: prove   15.67 s  proof   279.2 KB  ~mem   0.9 GB
  shard  2: prove   15.69 s  proof   279.2 KB  ~mem   0.9 GB
  shard  3: prove   15.45 s  proof   279.2 KB  ~mem   0.9 GB
  totals : sequential prove 62.5 s, combined proof 1116.9 KB (pre-aggregation), peak mem ~0.9 GB/shard
  shards are independent => embarrassingly parallel; each fits the per-shard memory
  bound regardless of total workload. Aggregating the 4 shard proofs into one
  succinct proof via FRI recursion is the remaining step (Plonky2/Plonky3 support it).
